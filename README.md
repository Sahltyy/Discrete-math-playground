# Discrete Lab

My discrete math study lab (Epp, *Discrete Mathematics with Applications*, 5th ed.):
sets, graphs, logic, proofs, number theory, sequences (§5.1) and induction (§5.2), with my professor's practice problems.

**Open it:** https://sahltyy.github.io/Discrete-math-playground/

## Use it offline on iPhone

1. Open the link above in **Safari** while you have internet.
2. Wait for the little "Saved for offline ✓" note.
3. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

After that, open it from the home-screen icon. It works with no signal. Progress is saved on the phone.

## What's in here

- `index.html` is the whole app
- `sw.js` keeps a copy on the phone for offline use
- `fonts/` has the fonts, bundled so nothing loads from the internet (Fontsource, SIL Open Font License)
- `icons/` has the home-screen icon
